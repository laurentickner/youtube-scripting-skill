# The "My Story / How I Made X" Video — Hero's Journey structure

A distinct video type with its own scripting logic, different from the tactical/tutorial scripts the rest of this skill covers. Source: YT Alchemy "MOD 2 — How I Made X" (Class 2.1.1 Script 1: Your Story). Full teacher transcript in `source-transcript-my-story-how-i-made-x.md`. Backbone: Joseph Campbell's **Hero's Journey** monomyth.

Related: Benji uses the same family of structure for Lauren's 128k authority video ("webinar-style, Hero's Journey"). This file is the full, fill-in-the-blank template for the *origin story* version.

---

## When to use

- The **storytelling / origin video** — "how I made [X]", "my story", the founder's-journey video.
- Usually the **first video in a channel sequence** ("your first video, the hardest one — everything gets easier after this").
- **Purpose = build a loyal community + brand, NOT the immediate sale.** This is the video that makes people trust and *love* you, so they'll monetize later. Identification with you, layered on top of your expertise, is what builds the loyal audience.

---

## The backbone — Hero's Journey as a triangle (3 acts)

- **Act 1 — Separation (the setup):** Ordinary World → Call to Adventure (the hook) → Refusal of the Call → Meeting the Mentor → Road of Trials.
- **Act 2 — Supreme Ordeal (the middle, split in two):** Cross the First Threshold → Approach the Inmost Cave → the Ordeal → the Reward ("fake acceptance") → Refusal of the Return → the Dark Night of the Soul.
- **Act 3 — Unification (resolution):** Cross the Third Threshold (Sacrifice / real action) → the *real* acceptance → Climax → Resurrection → Master of Two Worlds → Freedom to Live → and the circle spins again.

(Diagram = Lisa A. Paltz Spindler's monomyth map, drawn from Campbell's *Hero With A Thousand Faces* / *Power of Myth*, Vogler's *Writer's Journey*, Estes, Pollack, Ray.)

## The fill-in-the-blank script beats (in order)

Write it to the T, then swap the specifics for your true story and record. **Every beat connects to the next with a transition — never a jump.**

1. **HOOK / TRAILER** — exact date + biggest economic result + a plot twist.
   > *"In [year] I [founded / achieved big thing], which I [huge result — e.g. sold for over $1M]. But I ended up losing everything in a matter of [timeframe], even with [the debt / the damage]."*
   Big win → sudden loss. This is the movie trailer. Then deepen the bad situation.

2. **THE BEGINNINGS / CONTEXT (Ordinary World)** — how you started.
   > *"It all started when… In the middle of [X] I was working as a [previous profession], earning around [money], doing [routine]."*

3. **INFLECTION POINT (Call to Adventure)** — the last straw.
   > *"I remember how one day in [month] I decided to turn my life around, because [specific situation that made you rethink everything]."*
   Use the relatable hero-origin moment (e.g. "I saw my mum faint from overwork, and I knew something had to change"). Aim for the viewer to think *"does this sound familiar?"*
   - Mentality shift: *"I began to identify more with [X] and stopped identifying with [Z]."*
   - The decision: *"I made the decision to [decision]."*
   - First action: *"I started to investigate [options] to get out of the situation."*
   *(TRANSITION: "the adventure begins.")*

4. **CHOICES / FIRST ATTEMPTS (Road of Trials)** — first niches, mentors, routines.
   > *"By this time I'd discovered [choices]. The first was [drop shipping / whatever]. I tried it but only lasted [X months] — too complex for me."*
   Transition to a new niche → results → transition again → niche decision: *"I decided to stay in [niche] because it felt right."* (Most people churn through niches — this is relatable.)

5. **GOING DEEPER (new mentors / studies / habits)** — the light at the end of the tunnel.
   > *"Going deeper into [X], I started to see the opportunity, the hope came back. I felt I could make a full living from [X]. All of this led me to [X]."*

6. **FIRST RESULTS** — negative → average → small positive.
   > *"First the negative results, then average, then small improvements, then small but positive results."*

7. **THE EPIPHANY / THE MOMENT (the Reward / the great secret)** — the key to success.
   > *"I reached a great epiphany when I realised [the one key that let me take the first leap]. My first [client / win]. This is when I realised the only thing I had to do to get results was [X]."*
   Make the viewer feel their own version: *"I'm sure you're reading this and feeling that moment in your own life."*

8. **APPLYING THE SECRET + THE RISE (include EGO)** — the climb, then arrogance.
   > *"We applied the secret. By thinking and acting this way, using [strategy], that's where it all was born, and it led to real success. That's when I entered [new phase / scale] — new results, but also arrogance, carelessness, ego."*
   Deliberately include the ego / carelessness — it's human and it sets up the fall.

9. **THE FALL / THE EARTHQUAKE (Dark Night of the Soul)** — something destroys the plan.
   > *"But something happened. Something completely destroyed my plan. In a matter of [X days] I lost everything. There was something I was ignoring — [self-destructive action, e.g. drinking] — and it took its toll. It came from [root, e.g. my father]. I went into denial, depression, [struggle]."*
   Awareness → neglect → arrogance → catastrophe → denial. Don't sanitise it.

10. **STOICISM / THE TURNAROUND (the real acceptance / Resurrection)** — rock bottom → change.
    > *"But something happened. Stoicism. That's when I decided to turn everything around. I became me 2.0."*
    Transition into a better life.

11. **FINAL STAGE — lasting success + OPEN LOOP (the post-credit scene)** — Master of Two Worlds, left open.
    > *"Nowadays I'm smarter, [new results], with my personal milestones and goals — the next adventures."*
    **Never close it.** End on an opening: *"I beat [the lizard], but I'm still not with [Mary Jane], so I'm going to [next plan] — I'm building [a team]."* Like a Marvel post-credit scene.

## Core principles (non-negotiable)

- **Continuity — never break the flow.** Every beat is joined to the next by a transition. No jumps in the script; the copy never snaps.
- **Relatability / identification.** Emotional beats the viewer sees themselves in ("does this sound familiar?"). That identification, *on top of* your expertise, is what creates the loyal community.
- **Include the vulnerability AND the ego.** Arrogance, the fall, the self-destruction. This is what makes you human and the story believable. Sanitised = flat.
- **End on an OPEN loop, never closed.** Leave a little unsatisfaction / a next chapter, so viewers stay hooked and come back for the next piece and get engaged with the brand. A closed, fully-satisfied ending makes them stop caring. (Psychology: satisfied = they don't care; slightly unsatisfied = they chase the next piece.)
- **Purpose is brand + audience, not the sale.** After a viewer watches this whole story, there's no way they don't trust and love you. Monetisation comes later, off the back of that.

Pulled ~30 frames across the whole video and matched EVERY beat to the on-screen fill-in Google Doc. All 11 beats confirmed. The doc numbers the acts as "PERIODS" and uses explicit **"Transition:"** beats between sections (continuity made literal). The exact doc outline, in order:

- **1. PERIOD** (hook / trailer): *Exact date of the story* ("In 2020…") · *Largest result obtained at economic level ($)* ("I founded my first technology sales company which I sold after 5 months for more than 1M$") · *Plot twist* ("Losing everything") · *When did everything start to go wrong?* ("In a matter of 3 months") · *Deepening the bad situation* ("…even with a debt of half a million and death threats").
- **2. PERIOD** (beginnings / context) → the inflection: ordinary world → *the last straw* ("the specific situation that made you rethink everything") · *What changed in your mentality at that turning point?* · *What decision did you make?* · *How did you start taking action?* → Transition → **The adventure begins**.
- **Choices, first actions, first mentors, first routines**: *First Choice – First Niche – First Results* ("The first had been Dropshipping… only lasted # months… complex business model") → *Transition to new Niche* → *Niche decision* ("I decided to stay in this niche…").
- **Deepening – New Mentors, New Studies, New Habits, New Work Plan** → Transition.
- **First Results**: *Negative Results* → *Average Results* → *Small but positive results* ("already earning some money… at least it was profitable").
- **Epiphany – AHA MOMENT!** / **AHA Moment**: *The great secret* · *Key to success* ("the only thing I had to do to get results was X") · *Apply the secret* ("things started to change forever") · *Assured success* → Transition.
- **New Start – News Results**: *King Midas, Arrogance, Carelessness, Wastefulness* ("my Golden Era") · *New stage in my life* · *Money is no longer a problem* · *First whims* ("I bought my first Luxury item: Rolex – Sport") · *Enjoyment and pleasure* → Transition ("the Luxury lifestyle had a big lesson hidden in it").
- **The fall / earthquake**: *Awareness* · *Neglect (Self-destructive action)* · *Arrogance* · *Catastrophe* ("I lost # in # days") · *Denial* · *Frustration* · *Suffering*.
- **BUT – Stoicism**: monk mode, "analyze in a cold and calculated way", "only 3 specific actions were responsible for % of losses" → **PERSON 2.0**.
- **Final Stage + Real and Lasting Success**: *New philosophy* · *New results* · *Personal milestone* (left open — the next adventures / post-credit scene).
