# Intro Style Bank — Verified Exemplars

A bank of full-transcript intros from creators whose channels work in Lauren's lane (Dan Martell, Ed Lawrence). Use these as MODELS — pick the pattern that fits the video, then rewrite in Lauren's voice. Do NOT copy-paste structure mechanically.

Each intro is captured verbatim + tagged with the pattern shape so future scripting can match.

The default intro structure remains **Structure 1** (Hook → Break Belief → X2X → Credibility → Preview, from `copywriting-structures.md`). These exemplars show how that structure flexes in real verified videos.

---

## Pattern Index

1. **Compressed Structure 1** — Dan Martell #1, #2. 4-5 lines, beats run into each other, dive immediately. For tighter videos / faster ICPs.
2. **Full Structure 1 + paradigm-shift hook** — Dan Martell #3. Consensus reveal as hook ("all the experts are saying the same thing"). For higher-stakes / opportunity-led videos.
3. **Reframe hook + delayed credibility-as-result** — Ed Lawrence #1. "You've probably been told X" → challenge → credibility lands as a specific personal proof point woven into the promise.
4. **Question hook + data flex + multi-tease PREVIEW** — Ed Lawrence #2. Yes/no question → dramatic "I have the data" affirmation → multi-promise preview → credibility delayed via tool/method reveal.
5. **Hyperbole hook + hype + immediate dive** — Ed Lawrence #3. Listicle format. Loose structure. Hook is paradigm-shift + creator's own awe. No explicit credibility beat. Tool 1 starts inside the second minute.
6. **Structure 1 + extended teaching analogy** — Ed Lawrence #4. Full Structure 1, then an extended ANALOGY (the bar / two drinkers / whiskey) teaches the underlying tension before the system reveal. Pushes intro to ~2-3 min vs standard <60s — works when the analogy IS the belief-shift.

## Exemplar 1 — Dan Martell · "How to Get SO Many Customers with AI it feels ILLEGAL"

**Pattern:** Compressed Structure 1.

```
It's actually pretty easy to get more customers with AI
Because most people aren't plugging AI into their sales system the right way
I've trained 1000s of entrepreneurs on sales and today run a portfolio of companies that will generate 100 million dollars in revenue this year
And I can tell you, that's only possible because of how we're using AI to get new customers
So I'm gonna walk you through the 5 phases in sales and show you exactly how to use AI to get more customers whether you've got a full sales team or you're doing it solo
Let's start with the first phase, prospecting
```

**Beat map:**
- L1 HOOK — pattern-interrupt assertion ("it's actually easy")
- L2 BREAK BELIEF — names the mistake ("most people aren't plugging AI in the right way")
- L3 CREDIBILITY — "1000s of entrepreneurs" + "$100M revenue" portfolio
- L4 X2X — "only possible because of AI"
- L5 PREVIEW — "5 phases in sales" + inclusion clause ("whether full team or solo")
- L6 — immediate bridge to body ("Let's start with prospecting")

**Why it works:** 6 lines total. No fluff. Title-mirror on L1 ("more customers with AI"). Credibility is a single number ($100M). Audience inclusion ("whether full team or solo") expands TAM without softening promise.

```
Most people open Claude and use it like a fancy Google search

I'm gonna walk you through 16 claude hacks that'll save you hours of time, grow your business, and boost your productivity in ways that'll blow your freaking mind

```

**Beat map:**
- L1 HOOK — names the mistake
- L2 BREAK BELIEF — Ferrari analogy makes the mistake visceral
- L3 CREDIBILITY — "build AI products every single day"
- L4 X2X + PREVIEW — "16 claude hacks" + 3 specific outcomes

**Why it works:** 5 lines. Analogy does the heavy lifting in 1 line. Triple-outcome PREVIEW ("save you hours, grow your business, boost productivity"). The mid-sentence dive is intentional — kills the "pre-amble" feeling.

## Exemplar 3 — Dan Martell · "How to Build a $10M Solo AI Business (Zero Code)"

**Pattern:** Full Structure 1 + paradigm-shift hook.

```
All the biggest AI experts in the world are saying the same thing
The next wave of billion dollar companies won't have 100 employees, they won't have 50 employees, they'll have 1… 1 person using ai agents to do all the work
And if you do exactly what I'll teach you today, that could be you
I'm dan martell, i've built and sold multiple software companies, I've coached 1000s of people building AI businesses (some to 8 and 9 figure exits)
And what's happening right now with AI is the biggest opportunity I've seen since early 2000s
So in this video, I'm gonna show you how to completely rethink growing your business now that AI exists, the exact 6 step process to build a 1 person AI business from 0 to $10M+ maybe even billions AND how to get paid before you build anything
Let's start with rethinking business growth. Step number 1: stop throwing bodies at problems. The traditional business model is have an idea…
```

**Beat map:**
- L1 HOOK — consensus reveal ("the experts are saying the same thing")
- L2 BREAK BELIEF — paradigm flip ("100 → 50 → 1 employee")
- L3 X2X — "that could be you" (invites viewer into the new paradigm)
- L4 CREDIBILITY — "built and sold software" + "8 and 9 fig exits"
- L5 TIMING — "biggest opportunity I've seen since early 2000s"
- L6 PREVIEW — triple-promise ("rethink growth + 6-step process + get paid before build")
- L7 — dive into Step 1, mid-sentence

**Why it works:** Pulls authority by sharing it with "all the experts." Timing narrative ("biggest opportunity since 2000s") sits between credibility and preview — creates urgency without pressure. Triple-promise PREVIEW is a Dan Martell signature.

## Exemplar 4 — Ed Lawrence · "The new way to make $1M from YouTube Using AI"

**Pattern:** Reframe hook + delayed credibility-as-result.

```
So, you've probably been told that the best way to make a million dollars from YouTube is to build a course, or to start a community, or to hire a sales team. If your goal is to build a business that generates consistent income without burning out, I don't believe those models are the best option for everyone anymore.
So, in this video, I'm going to show you the big problem with courses and high-ticket programs that very few people talk about, and then I'm going to show you a new way of making millions from YouTube with AI that solves most of those issues, and it might be more sustainable and profitable for you.
In fact, I recently launched this new model, and it generated $96,000 in 40 minutes with no sales calls. So, to understand why this new model works, we first need to look at the big problem with courses and programs that everyone tells you to build. Okay, so we'll start with the problems with the low-ticket model.
```

**Beat map:**
- HOOK — "you've probably been told" + list of common advice (course / community / sales team)
- BREAK BELIEF — soft denial ("I don't believe those models are the best option")
- PREVIEW — two-part promise ("big problem + new way")
- CREDIBILITY-AS-RESULT — "$96,000 in 40 minutes with no sales calls" (one specific number, recent, irrefutable)
- BRIDGE TO BODY — "to understand why this works, we first need to look at the big problem..."

**Why it works:** Credibility is NOT "I've been doing this for 10 years." It's ONE specific recent result that proves the new model. The reframe is soft ("for everyone anymore") which makes it feel reasonable, not contrarian-for-sake-of-it. The bridge frames the body as necessary education ("we need to look at the problem first").

## Exemplar 5 — Ed Lawrence · "What I Learned Tracking Sales From 1 BILLION Views"

**Pattern:** Question hook + data flex + multi-tease PREVIEW + delayed credibility.

```
Does the topic, title, and style of your video impact how many sales that you're getting from YouTube? Well, the answer is yes, dramatically. And now, for the first time ever, I have the data to prove it after tracking sales from 1 billion YouTube views. So, in this video, I'm going to show you the patterns we found in this data that are increasing channel sales.
I'm going to show you the title formats that are making YouTube channels the most money. You're going to see if the style of video you're making right now is actually costing you sales. And I'm going to show you exactly what the top channels are doing from everyone else to make millions. And at the end, I'm going to show you why using emojis in your title might actually increase your income.
Okay, so before we get into the data, let me just quickly show you where it's all come from, cuz you're probably wondering, how has he tracked sales from a billion views? Sounds like internet marketing nonsense. Well, this is Rev Track. It's a software we built at the start of 2025, and it tracks individual sales, views, and leads from YouTube and other platforms, too.
```

**Beat map:**
- HOOK — yes/no question that the viewer already wonders ("does X impact Y?")
- AFFIRM + DATA FLEX — "yes, dramatically. 1 billion views"
- PREVIEW — multi-tease (5 specific promises: patterns / title formats / video style cost / what top channels do / emoji surprise)
- DELAYED CREDIBILITY — "Rev Track... software we built" (proves the data is real)
- BRIDGE — "so before we get into the data..."

**Why it works:** The question hook makes the viewer agree with you before you've said anything. The data flex (1B views) is the credibility — bigger than any personal-result number. The multi-tease PREVIEW creates 5 reasons to stay (each one a separate retention loop). Delayed credibility via tool reveal handles the "this sounds like marketing nonsense" objection BEFORE the viewer thinks it.

## Exemplar 6 — Ed Lawrence · "5 NEW AI That Just Changed YouTube Forever"

**Pattern:** Hyperbole + hype + immediate dive.

```
The biggest leap in AI has just happened. And it's now possible for YouTube channels to create content in hours that used to take experienced teams weeks. And the businesses that jump on the AI I'm about to show you are going to see exponential results. Honestly, I cannot believe what I'm about to show you is real.
Okay, so this first AI, this isn't the most mind-blowing of the bunch, but I want to start with it because it's a vastly improved version of an image creator that allows you to create thumbnail images that look like this in a matter of seconds...
```

**Beat map:**
- HOOK — paradigm-shift hyperbole ("biggest leap")
- AMPLIFY — "hours vs weeks" specific contrast
- TIMING — "businesses that jump on this will see exponential results"
- AUTHENTICITY — "Honestly, I cannot believe what I'm about to show you is real"
- Dive into Tool 1 with pre-empt — "this isn't the most mind-blowing, but..."

**Why it works:** No explicit credibility. The hook IS the credibility ("biggest leap" + "I cannot believe this is real" = Ed has seen enough AI to know this is different). The Tool-1 pre-empt ("not the most mind-blowing but...") manages expectations and builds a retention loop (implication: better ones are coming).

**When to use:** listicle / tool-roundup videos where the title makes the promise clear. Skip the credibility beat because the LIST is the credibility.

## Exemplar 7 — Ed Lawrence · "EXACTLY How to make sales from EVERY video you make"

**Pattern:** Structure 1 + extended teaching analogy (~2-3 min intro).

```
Your YouTube videos won't make you money if you are obsessing over attention and likes and subscribers. And I know this because I've helped over 700 businesses make millions on YouTube, and this year alone my own channel pulled in over 4.5 million dollars. So, in this video, I'm going to give you the five-step system that I use in pretty much every video I make that generates sales every time I post one.

All right, so to understand this system, you need to know how making money on YouTube actually works. Otherwise, it's not going to work for you. And the best way to explain this is I want you to imagine that you run a bar, and your job is to get everyone inside to drink Johnny Walker's cheapest whiskey all night.

Now, it doesn't sound that hard, right? But, the thing you're going to quickly realize is there are two types of drinkers. The first are a very small group of whiskey fanatics. So, if you pour them a Johnny and Coke, they look at you like you just spat in their mother's face because they've had that basic whiskey a thousand times before, and they think it's lame.

And these people actually came to your bar hoping for something more unique with real depth. And the moment you don't give them that, they leave. And then the second type of drinker, these are the complete opposite. They can drink whiskey Cokes all night, but if you gave them a neat 100-year-old single malt, they would take one sip, and they would pull the face of someone who's eaten a bowl of bees because it would be too strong and it would overwhelm them, so they'd spit it out.

And that's why trying to make videos that get lots of views and then lots of sales at the same time doesn't really work because if you want views, you have to make more watered-down content that the wider market here can consume without getting overwhelmed. But, the thing is that watered-down experience repels your buyers because they, like the whiskey fanatics, want something much deeper.

The more serious viewers here are also at the stage where they're ready to pay someone to help them. Okay, so now I'm going to give you my five-step system for making videos that generate sales every time you post one. And then after, I'm going to show you how to make videos that get views and sales cuz it is still possible.

All right, so step one of this system works like this. If I want to make sales, I do not make a listicle, I do not make an interview video, or I do not make a news-style update...
```

**Beat map:**
- Para 1 — Structure 1 condensed: HOOK + CREDIBILITY ("700 businesses + $4.5M") + PREVIEW ("5-step system")
- Para 2 — BRIDGE to analogy ("to understand this system, you need to know how it actually works")
- Paras 3-6 — EXTENDED ANALOGY: bar / two types of drinkers / whiskey-Coke vs neat single malt
- Para 7 — APPLY the analogy back to YouTube (this is the BELIEF SHIFT lands here, not in the intro)
- Para 8 — RE-PROMISE the 5-step system + secondary promise (views AND sales)
- Para 9 — Dive into Step 1

**Why it works:** The analogy IS the break-belief, and it earns its 90 seconds because:
1. It primes the viewer's brain with a familiar scenario (bartending)
2. It makes them ARRIVE at the tension themselves (you can't serve both audiences at once)
3. When you apply it back to YouTube, the viewer already agrees
4. It separates "this works for everyone" thinkers from "I'd rather get sales" thinkers — selecting for the right viewer for the rest of the video

**When to use:** When the body's core thesis depends on the viewer holding a specific mental model (e.g. two-types-of-viewers / two-types-of-buyers). Don't try to argue them into the model — drop them in via story.

## Cross-pattern observations

- **5 of 7 intros start with a stated belief that the viewer already half-suspects**, then dismantle it. Hooks that align with the viewer's existing scepticism beat hooks that try to manufacture surprise.
- **Credibility is ONE number or ONE result**, never a CV recital. "$100M portfolio." "$96,000 in 40 minutes." "700 businesses + $4.5M this year." "1 billion views tracked." The viewer remembers ONE number; don't waste the slot on three.
- **PREVIEW is always specific**. Generic ("you'll learn how to grow") is dead. Specific ("16 hacks" / "5 phases" / "6 step process" / "patterns + title formats + emoji surprise") survives.
- **Mid-sentence dives into the body**. Six of seven examples end the intro by starting the body — no "OK, let's get into it" pre-amble. The dive itself signals the value is starting.
- **Audience-inclusion lines** ("whether you've got a full sales team or you're doing it solo") expand TAM without softening the promise. Use sparingly — once per intro max.
- **Timing narratives** (Dan #3: "biggest opportunity since 2000s"; Ed #6: "businesses that jump on this will see exponential results") fit naturally between credibility and preview.

## How to use this file

When scripting a new intro:

1. Identify the pattern that fits the video. Use the Pattern Index above.
2. Read the matching exemplar in full.
3. Note the beat map for the pattern.
4. Draft Lauren's intro using the SAME beat sequence + similar beat lengths + her own voice and content.
5. Run the standard R-A audit (`SKILL.md` R-A rule) against the matching exemplar's structure as the rubric.

Do not copy creator-specific language (e.g. "watered-down content," "Phantom Sales Team for Dan," "Rev Track for Ed"). Lift structure, not phrasing.
